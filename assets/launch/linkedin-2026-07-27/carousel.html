What happens
after you set it up

1

You say “publish this”

Your AI publishes the document and hands you back a link. No copy-pasting, no export step.

2

You send the link

Share it with named people, with everyone on your company domain, or with anyone who has the link.

3

They comment on the exact line

Readers highlight the sentence they mean and leave a note there. Reading needs no account.

4

Your AI reads the comments and revises

It publishes a new version to the same link. Nobody has to hunt for the latest file.
